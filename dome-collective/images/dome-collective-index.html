07 The Edge

The land structure is the moat.

  • 15-year ground lease on the 9-acre parcel at just $8,000/mo (2% annual escalator)
  • A $10M option to purchase the land before year 8 — the residences carry $7M (70%) of it, with the retreat / Within taking the remaining $3M for their share of the parcel
  • During the lease, the landowner carries the land taxes — we only pay on our improvements, keeping carrying costs low through construction and lease-up
  • When we exercise, we lock the land beneath a ~$27M stabilized asset — and capture that spread for our equity, not the landowner's

Lock $7M land beneath a $27M stabilized asset — the spread is ours.
